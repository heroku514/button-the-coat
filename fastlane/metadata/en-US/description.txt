Button the Coat keeps one pretend coat on the phone. It starts open. Shut the bottom button first, then the middle, then the top. A higher button stays open until the ones below it are shut. Open only the top shut button. The huge line says the coat is open, the bottom is shut, two buttons are shut, or the coat is warm.

The coat stays on this phone. Start a new coat when you want every button open again.

Button the Coat does not use an account, ads, a camera, a clock, or a network connection. It does not button a real coat. The coat in the app is original.
